# NEO — AI Study System

**An AI-powered study tool built with Python to help students turn their notes into effective study materials and track their learning progress.**

## Overview

NEO is a personal AI study system designed to make studying more organized, efficient, and consistent. It takes lecture notes and uses AI to generate summaries and flashcards, allowing students to practise what they learn and track their performance over time.

The goal is to build a reliable study tool that saves time on preparation and helps students focus on understanding and remembering information.

## The Problem

Students often spend significant time manually summarizing lecture notes, creating flashcards, and organizing study materials. They may also struggle to maintain consistent study habits or identify which topics they need to review.

NEO aims to bring these tasks together in one system.

## Planned Features

- **Note Management:** Import, organize, and access lecture notes.
    
- **AI-Generated Summaries:** Turn lecture notes into concise summaries.
    
- **AI-Generated Flashcards:** Generate question-and-answer flashcards based on supplied notes.
    
- **Interactive Study Sessions:** Practise flashcards one question at a time and assess answers.
    
- **Progress Tracking:** Record correct and incorrect answers, calculate accuracy, and identify difficult flashcards.
    
- **Persistent Storage:** Save notes, study materials, and practice history so users can return to previous sessions.
    
- **Error Handling:** Handle invalid input, API failures, and storage errors reliably.
